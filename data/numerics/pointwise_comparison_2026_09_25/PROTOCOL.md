# Pointwise statistical protocol
The machine-readable PROTOCOL.json specifies the analysis before its new bootstrap outputs. This is retrospective: the sample, model choices and existing losses were already examined.

Primary inference uses the continuous pointwise squared standardized error, averages it within each galaxy and compares the mean paired difference across whole galaxies. All radial observations remain in each resampled galaxy. Four finite full-sample comparisons form the declared mean-loss testing family. Profiled baryons have a training failure and cannot receive a finite full-sample continuous-loss p-value.

Report effects, resampling intervals, wild-cluster p-values and Monte Carlo resolution together. Correlation assumptions, alternative weighting, absolute-error scores and every galaxy's influence are sensitivities, not alternative paths to a selected significance. Do not interpret any result as fresh validation or evidence specifically for cubic field dynamics.
